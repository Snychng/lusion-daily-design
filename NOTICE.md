`reference/` preserves the public published runtime and the resources used for local comparison. The Astro output, Three.js r158 runtime, shaders, scene geometry, astronaut, animation, textures, fonts, audio and original project media are attributed to their original authors. Original notices in published files remain intact. The [source manifest](分析/20261010-live/asset-manifest.json) records URLs, publication metadata and SHA-256 fingerprints.

The Daily Design content adaptation, procedural D geometry, conceptual particle forms, sticker atlas, scripts and local media mapping are documented in the [README](README.md), [model notes](src/models/README.md) and [media audit](brand-assets/media/media-audit.json). Existing third-party material is not represented as newly authored work.
